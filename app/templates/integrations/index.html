{# One field that matters. Everything a calendar service calls this - "secret address in iCal format", "public address", "subscribe" - is the same .ics URL, and nothing is ever sent back, so which one you paste only changes how much it shows. #}

Paste the calendar's address in iCal format — the link ending in .ics that Google, iCloud, Outlook or Nextcloud give you under "subscribe" or "secret address". The public and the private one both work; the private one simply shows more. Nothing is ever written back to the calendar — this only reads.
